## How to run things

Use Godot 4.7.2 (the `_console.exe` build on Windows for command-line runs). The project is `game\project.godot`.

| Command (add after the Godot exe: `--path game`) | What it does |
|---|---|
| *(nothing)* | Play the game |
| `--headless -- --autotest` | Runs the 49 gameplay checks |
| `-- --groundcheck` | Confirms nothing floats; **must run windowed**, not headless |
| `-- --capture=<dir> [--only=name] [--fish] [--hud]` | Takes screenshots of fixed views and reports FPS |

**Keep the Godot editor closed** while I process models. It locks files and re-extracts textures.

## Session 4 (2026-10-12): recorded sound effects

You generated 61 ElevenLabs takes into `audio_src/`. **`tools/build_sfx.py`** turns them into the game's sounds (`python tools/build_sfx.py`; `gen_audio.py` also runs it last, so the old synthetic versions never come back). It decodes each take, removes low rumble, finds each footfall's exact attack, then cuts it with a 12 ms lead-in and a soft fade-out and brings every take to the same loudness.

- **Footsteps** (`step_<surface>_N`):
  - dirt: 8 takes
  - sand: 6
  - wood: 6
  - grass: 4
  - stone: 6, made from the dirt takes with the thump removed
  - The "soft grass at night" takes turned out to be one continuous rustle with a low hum and no separate steps, so the grass steps come from the clean grass hits in your jump-on-grass takes.
- **Jumps and landings:**
  - `jump_grass_N` is the rustle as your feet push off.
  - `jump_wood_N`, `land_grass_N` and `land_wood_N` are matched to what you're standing on.
  - Dirt, sand and stone use a heavier, lower step instead.
- **Matched to movement:**
  - **Pace:** one footfall every 1.2 m, about 2.5 a second at walking speed (it was 3.5, which sounded like running).
  - **Starting and stopping:** the first step sounds as soon as you start walking, and the step counter resets when you land.
  - **Jumps:** the push-off sound plays on the jump and the landing on touchdown.
  - **Drops:** stepping off a ledge plays an ordinary step.
  - **Surface:** wood on the dock, bench and bridge; dirt on paths; sand at the pond edge and stream banks; grass everywhere else. The autotest checks this.
- **Water:**
  - `loop_water` is 4 calm-pond takes crossfaded into a 12 s seamless loop.
  - `loop_stream` is 4 stream takes in a 6.2 s loop.
  - Both are lightly compressed so no single splash jumps out.
  - `splash_N` and `plop_N` (the sharp first drop of a splash) come from your fish-splash takes.
  - `thrash_N` (a hooked fish churning) comes from your fish-float takes.
- **Fishing:**
  - `whoosh_N` is the rod swish, from the cast takes.
  - `line_out` is the line paying out. It plays during the cast and again when the fish takes line.
  - `loop_reel` is the reel ratchet. It loops only while you hold to reel and drops in pitch while the fish pulls.
  - Surges play a thrash.
- **Wildlife and bench:** `frog_1..4`, `owl_1..3`, and `sit_1` (bench creak).
- **Variant takes:** `Sfx` now plays a random take for any cue with numbered takes, never the same take twice in a row. It also has `variant_count()` and `has_clip()`.
- **Autotest:** 60/60, including footstep surfaces and a check that every take is present. The bench test now waits for the catch to fully settle.
