## Mix and implementation

Master, Music, SFX, and Ambience buses with persisted sliders. Keep water and key interaction cues audible beneath music. Use spatial sources for world loops, nonspatial audio for essential UI feedback. Visual bite feedback must remain usable with audio muted. Leave headroom, remove clicks from loops, and limit concurrent decorative sounds. Pause must not allow gameplay timers to advance while audio creates misleading feedback.

Use original or appropriately licensed music/recordings and keep attribution with the build. No voice acting is required; one or two lines of readable dialogue and quiet character sounds are enough.

## Copy-ready music direction

Create an original seamless cozy instrumental loop for a magical lantern garden at blue hour. Felt piano, soft plucked strings, warm airy pad, sparse small bell accents, approximately 70 BPM. Gentle curiosity and comfort, no vocals, no busy percussion, no dramatic trailer build. Keep the melody spacious so water and fishing cues remain clear. Also create a 20–30 second related ending variation that warmly resolves without becoming loud or bombastic. Export clean stereo files and document usage rights.
